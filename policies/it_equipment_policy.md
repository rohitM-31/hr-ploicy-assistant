# IT Equipment Policy v1.5

## Laptop
Every employee receives one company laptop. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years.
Lost or damaged laptops must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Accessories
Employees can claim a monitor, keyboard and mouse for home setup up to INR 10,000, once every 3 years.

## Return of Equipment
All company equipment, including the laptop, must be returned on the last working day. Unreturned equipment cost is deducted from final settlement.
