# Remote Work Policy v2.0

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period can work remotely up to 2 days per week.
Employees on probation must work from office all 5 days.

## Approval
Remote work days must be approved by the reporting manager in the HR portal.
Fully remote work requires approval from both the department head and HR.

## Working Hours
Remote employees must be available online between 10 AM and 5 PM IST.
