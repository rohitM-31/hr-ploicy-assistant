# Leave Policy v3.2

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave must be applied in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance.
Up to 6 unused days can be carried forward to the next year. Remaining days lapse on 31 December.

## Sick Leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 5 working days, to be used within 3 months of the child's birth.
