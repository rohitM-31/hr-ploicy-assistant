# Expense Policy v4.1

## Travel Expenses
Domestic flights are reimbursed in economy class only.
Hotel stay is reimbursed up to INR 5,000 per night in metro cities and INR 3,500 in other cities.

## Meals
Meal and food expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to INR 1,200 per day.
Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Claim Submission
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense with original bills.
Claims above INR 25,000 need finance head approval.
